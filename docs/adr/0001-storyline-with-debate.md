# Tell the talk as one invented company's story, with a debate as its climax

The talk follows **PortoBikes**, an invented startup, through six **turning points**: ClickOps → scripts → Terraform (with the live demo) → team growth → the license change → the future. Each turning point is backed by a **real anchor**. One argument runs through the whole story: *Terraform made infrastructure reviewable code; that is why it became the standard, and why the BSL change and the IBM acquisition matter.* At turning point 5, the three speakers debate Terraform vs OpenTofu vs Pulumi, and the class votes before and after.

## Considered Options

- **Pure argument** (mechanism → adoption → turmoil): it covered every question in the brain dump, but had no storyline to make the history and the future hang together.
- **Whole talk as a debate** ("Is Terraform already legacy?"): memorable, but every speaker would need deep knowledge of the competitors, and the demo would have no natural place. We kept the debate as one sharp moment instead.
- Others rejected early: centred on `plan` only (no room for the business story), framed around the job market (too shallow technically), an open-source business case study (too far from DevOps).

## Consequences

The company is invented, so credibility depends on saying so on the first slide and tagging every real anchor with its source.
